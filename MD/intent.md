# Intent

Give an organisation one place to know where every piece of IT hardware is, who holds it, what it has cost to keep running, and what happened to it at the end of its life.

## Who it is for
- **Admin**: registers assets and master data, runs the lifecycle actions.
- **Manager**: watches status, maintenance and archive without being able to change anything.
- **Employee (User)**: sees the assets assigned to them and their history.

## What it must guarantee
- An asset is always in exactly one lifecycle state, and only valid transitions are possible.
- Every hand-over is recorded in the ownership ledger.
- Disposal is documented (reason, method, certificate number).
- People see only what their role allows.
